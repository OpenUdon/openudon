# M87: a public step-authoring command contract owned by OpenUdon

Approved plan, 2026-09-26. The commands are not implemented or accepted yet.

[M87's retirement record](../docs/history/status-M87.md) preserves the approved
specification and original six-row status, which established a new public
non-interactive authoring contract. OpenUdon drafts the request/result schemas,
compatibility rules and initial fixtures first, independently of Kinet
M05/A03/W03. Kinet's first W03 row supplies consumer requirements and adapter
feedback; its later real-tool check consumes OpenUdon's published contract.

The commands reuse existing intent, source/auth, transactional writing and
advisory review logic. Step checks separate deterministic compatibility from
unresolved intent questions. Bind changes one explicitly identified step,
preserves unrelated content, and rejects stale or mismatched inputs before
writing. Candidates retain source identity, OR-of-AND auth needs and explicit
effect evidence; unknown effects are conservative and HTTP methods alone do
not establish read authority. Flow review remains read-only and advisory,
with model failure or unavailability visible in its result.

Apitools owns the missing consumer-summary/effect/step-contract-ranking
capabilities. Contract and fixture work can advance immediately; production
candidates integration and complete M87 acceptance require a compatible
published Apitools API. Fixture adapters are not delivered integration, and
OpenUdon must not duplicate generic metadata policy to evade that dependency.

The new contract is additive. Existing iCoT surfaces, package gates and UWS
semantics retain their meaning. Kinet owns confirmations, receipts, planning,
repair orchestration and ledger placeholders. S2b simulation, supervised
browser acquisition and package-level pending steps, plus S3 retirement after
consumer migration, remain unnumbered candidates. No sibling files, current
product/architecture/stack facts, retired history, or goal-launch input change
as part of these planning actions.
