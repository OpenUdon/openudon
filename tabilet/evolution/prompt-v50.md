## Requested direction

Browser work becomes provisional Stage 12; the former provisional Stages 11/12 become Stages 13/14 by explicit one-time user instruction. Completed stages and milestone identities are unchanged.

OpenUdon:P09 owns supported public v3 construction and source/shape verification; isolation belongs to the consuming worker. Kinet becomes the primary non-browser authoring product in Phase B. Public OpenUdon never imports private Udon modules; browser-dependent compatibility code remains until the Stage 12 closure gate.

Success requires supported shared contracts, direct Kinet authoring, isolated library execution, explicit migration/fresh approval and a qualified default-closed release.
