`openudon.browser-profile-transaction.v1` contract for immutable BAP+BCP and
BRP candidate provenance, review, preparation, promotion, cancellation,
failure, and indeterminate recovery. Authentication plus capability composes
one execution-local symbolic session; registration remains session-free and
fails before executor invocation.

in-memory virtual sources with exact source, review, result, freshness, and

restrictive scratch qualification, and atomic selected/prior generation

selector, or deletes the target, selected, or prior generation.

UI, and the compatible terminal flow with separate revision-bound review,
prepare, promote, inspect, recover, and cancel decisions. Runtime, sign-in,
credential, account, and registration-submit routes remain closed.
