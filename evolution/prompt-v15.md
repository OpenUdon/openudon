# Prompt V15 - Value-Free Browser Verification Packaging

Let operators attach Browsertools current-page and portability reports to an
exact reviewed browser profile without turning OpenUdon into a browser or a raw
evidence store. Accept only the two value-free report contracts, independently
reconstruct their profile-derived checks, detect replacement at approval, and
carry safe facts through package quality, human review, digest, and trusted
handoff.

Keep report files, local paths, page values, backend errors, private rich
evidence, authentication material, sessions, and runtime authority outside the
package. Absence remains valid, and portability remains optional confidence.
